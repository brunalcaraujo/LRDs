Description of storchibergmann_lrds_2026.csv (Storchi Bergmann et al. 2026)
This file contains 78 entries.

Column              Unit            Description
pid                 -               JWST program ID
filename            -               DJA filename
z                   -               Spectroscopic redshift
norm_factor         -               Normalization factor: median of the spectra within the 3446-3646 AA spectral window
Ha_Hb_ratio         -               Total (broad+narrow) Halpha/Hbeta ratio
Ha_Hb_ratio_err     -               Uncertainty on the total Halpha/Hbeta ratio
logL5100            log erg/s       Optical luminosity lambda*L_lambda at lambda = 5100 AA
logL5100_err        log erg/s       Uncertainty on log L_5100
OIII_Hbeta_ratio    -               Ratio of [O III] luminosity to Hbeta luminosity
OIII_Hbeta_ratio_err -              Uncertainty on L_[OIII]/L_Hbeta
F5500N              -               Mean continuum flux measured around 5500 AA on the normalized spectra
F5500N_err          -               Uncertainty on the mean continuum flux at 5500 AA
bin                 -               Bin assigned for the spectra in this work (B1-B5)
absorption          -               Flag indicating the presence of absorption features in the spectrum (True/False)
absorption_ref      -               Paper reference(s) reporting absorption features in the spectrum


The LRD spectra were retrieved from the public DAWN JWST Archive and originate from the following JWST programs:

CAPERS (GO-6368), PI: Dickinson (16 spectra)
UNCOVER (GO-2561), Bezanson et al. 2024, Price et al. 2025 (10 spectra)

JADES (GTO-1180, GTO-1181, GTO-1286), Curtis-Lake et al. 2025, Scholtz et al. 2025 (10 spectra)
NEXUS (GO-5105), Shen et al. 2024 (8 spectra)
Mirage or Miracle (GO-5224), PIs: Oesch & Naidu (3 spectra)
GO-2198, PI: Barrufet (3 spectra)
GO-4106, PIs: Nelson & Labbe (3 spectra)
DDT-6585, PI: Coulter (2 spectra)
CANUCS (GTO-1208), Sarrouh et al. 2025 (2 spectra)
GTO-WIDE (GTO-1212, GTO-1213, GTO-1215), Maseda et al. 2024 (4 spectra)

GO-1433, PI: Coe (1 spectrum)
